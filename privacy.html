Privacy Policy

The Short Version

Party Playbox has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you type and choose stays on your phone. I only ever get what you choose to send me, like a feedback email.

Who I Am

Party Playbox is made by Yuvaraj K, an independent developer in India. "I" and "me" in this policy mean the developer. For any question about your privacy, email angelstellar73@gmail.com.

What Stays on Your Phone

To work the way you left it, the app saves these on your phone:

  • Player names, saved groups and the names on Spin the Wheel.
  • Your settings and each game's defaults.
  • Your favourites, Home's layout and your widgets' setup.
  • Which words and questions came up lately, so they don't repeat too soon.
  • That someone confirmed they're 18 or older.
  • A record of your plan, so it works without the internet.

The app never sends any of this to me or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it.

Your Phone's Backup

Android can include the app's saved data in your phone's backup to your Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

What Google Play Handles

Plans, donations and refunds all go through Google Play. I never see your card or payment details. For each order, Google shares with me what it shares with every developer, such as the order number, the product, the price and the country, and I use that only for payments, refunds and taxes.

Pictures for some categories download from Google Play when they're needed. Google Play's billing and download tools may send Google basic technical information to make purchases and downloads work, under Google's privacy policy.

Motion and Voice

Tilt It reads your phone's motion sensor to tell when the phone is on someone's forehead and which way it tilts. Timer Impostor can use your phone's own text-to-speech voice to say Start and Stop. Both work only on your phone. The app never uses your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

When You Email Me

Send Feedback opens your email app with a draft that includes the app's version, your phone's model and its Android version, so I can fix problems on phones like yours. You can delete those before sending.

I get only what you send: your email address, your message and anything in it. I use it only to reply and to improve the app, never share or sell it, and delete it when it's no longer needed, within two years at the most. Ask me any time and I'll delete it sooner.

Links That Leave the App

Rate on Google Play, Tell a Friend, a photo's source and the links on Meet the Developer open other apps and websites. What happens there is covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

Party Playbox isn't made for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. The 18+ and Couples categories ask players to confirm they're 18 or older before they open.

Your Rights

Wherever you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and Europe's GDPR, you can ask me what I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Since the app keeps nothing about you, that only ever means emails you've sent me. Email me and I'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be here and on the app's Google Play page, with a new date below. If the app is ever going to collect something new, it will tell you in the app first.
