# Party Playbox: Privacy Policy and Terms

The public Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions for **Party Playbox**, a box of party games to play on one phone with friends, on Android.

- Privacy Policy: https://yuvarajk2109.github.io/party-playbox-legal/privacy.html
- Terms and Conditions: https://yuvarajk2109.github.io/party-playbox-legal/terms.html

These are the same words the app shows under Settings, About. In short, Party Playbox has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking: what you type and choose stays on your phone, and payments go through Google Play.

## How this site is made

The pages are generated from the app's own text, so the app and this site always say the same thing. Don't edit the HTML here by hand: change the text in the app, regenerate the pages there and copy them over.
