# Party Playbox website

The website for **Party Playbox**, a box of party games to play on one phone with friends, on Android: its games, and its Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

- Home: https://yuvarajk2109.github.io/party-playbox-legal/
- Privacy Policy: https://yuvarajk2109.github.io/party-playbox-legal/privacy.html
- Terms and Conditions: https://yuvarajk2109.github.io/party-playbox-legal/terms.html

The policy and terms are the same words the app shows under Settings, About. In short, Party Playbox has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking: what you type and choose stays on your phone, and payments go through Google Play.

## How this site is made

The pages are generated from the app itself (its words, game drawings and colours), so the app and this site always match. Don't edit the HTML here by hand: change the text in the app, regenerate the pages there and copy them over.
